# iOS app (SwiftUI)

Native SwiftUI app for iPhone (iOS 17+, Swift 6), offline-first with live round sync. SwiftData is the local store. See [../docs/architecture.md](../docs/architecture.md) for the client design and conflict strategy.

## Commands

```
open ios/Wad.xcodeproj          # build and run from Xcode
ios/scripts/test.sh             # build + every test on the first available iPhone simulator
ios/scripts/test.sh --unit      # the unit tests only (WadTests); what CI's unit-tests job runs
ios/scripts/test.sh --ui        # the UI tests only (WadUITests); what CI's ui-tests job runs
ios/scripts/test.sh -only-testing:WadTests/RoundStoreTests   # a single suite
```

Anything after the flag goes to xcodebuild, such as `-resultBundlePath`.

## Project file

The Xcode project is generated by [XcodeGen](https://github.com/yonaskolb/XcodeGen) from `project.yml`. The generated `Wad.xcodeproj` is committed, so you only need XcodeGen when adding targets or changing build settings:

```
cd ios && xcodegen
```

Edit `project.yml` rather than the project in Xcode's target settings, or the change is lost on the next regeneration. New Swift files under `Wad/` or `WadTests/` are picked up by regenerating.

## Layout

- `Wad/` — app target: `WadApp` (entry point and SwiftData container), `RootView` (tab shell), `Models/` (SwiftData models and their mapping to the engine inputs), `Setup/` (new-round flow: `RoundDraft` with its validation, and the course, players and games steps; the games step's "Start every hole at par", on by default, creates the round with par saved for every player on every hole, so the round is complete from the start and the scores are changed as it is played), `Rounds/` (the round history and round detail with progress, scorecard and the players' Venmo handles; a score tapped on the scorecard opens `ScoreEditSheet`, the scoring screen's stepper for that one score, which closes itself when a game event's show starts so the show is not hidden behind it, and the Out, In and Total sums cannot be tapped), `History/` (`RoundSummary`, what a history row says about a round, and the in-memory cache that keeps the engines from running for every row on every render), `Scoring/` (`RoundScorer` records scores, Wad makers and greenies and saves each change; `RoundStatus` is the engines' view of the round; the hole-by-hole scoring screen, which pops up the way to the settlement when a change completes the round or is made on the last hole of a round that is complete, as a round started at par is, and once dismissed stays away until another hole is visited; `RoundCompletionPrompt` is that rule, and `AppNavigation.push` hands the push to `RoundsView`, which holds the path), `Settlement/` (`RoundSettlement` is what the settlement screen shows: the engine's payments, net positions and per-game results, provisional until all 18 holes are scored; an unresolved skins carryover is shown and never paid), `Payments/` (Venmo handles, the Venmo pay and request links, and the paid markers; see Payments below), `Wolf/` (the Wolf game: its setup settings, the tee order, the per-hole records on the scorer, the engine's result mapped for the scoring and settlement screens, and their sections; see Wolf below), `Theme/` (the design system: the colors, which are in the asset catalog with a light and a dark variant, type, spacing, the reusable pieces such as the card, the section header, the stat pill, the money label and the button styles, the scorecard marks of `ScoreNotation` and the illustration of the empty states), `Engine/` (`EngineBridge`, which runs the TypeScript game engines in JavaScriptCore), `Resources/engines.js` (generated by `npm run build:ios-engines` in `backend/`; do not edit)
- `WadTests/` — unit tests (Swift Testing)
- `WadUITests/` — three XCUITest smoke tests, each a few minutes on CI, attaching a screenshot of each screen: `RoundWalkthroughUITests` sets up a round by tapping and typing (turning off "Start every hole at par") and scores its first three holes; `SettlementPaymentsUITests` pays and requests with Venmo on a seeded finished round (checking the link, without opening Venmo), marks payments paid and not paid, and checks the history row; `RoundDeleteUITests` deletes a round from the list, including a relaunch on a store on disk. The settlement amounts, the setup validation and the Venmo links are checked by the unit tests
- `scripts/test.sh` — picks an available iPhone simulator so the same command works locally and in CI; it first runs `scripts/test_select_device.py`. `--unit` and `--ui` run one of the two test targets; CI (`.github/workflows/ios-ci.yml`) runs them as the jobs `unit-tests` and `ui-tests`
- `scripts/make-app-icon.swift` — draws the app icon with CoreGraphics and writes the light, dark and tinted 1024 x 1024 PNGs of `AppIcon.appiconset` (`swift ios/scripts/make-app-icon.swift`)
- `scripts/install-device.sh` — builds for the iPhone that can be reached and installs the app. `scripts/select_device.py` picks the phone from `xcrun devicectl list devices --json-output`; a phone that is paired but unavailable is not used, and the script says to unlock it and connect it by USB or the same Wi-Fi

Debug builds accept the launch argument `-debugSetupStep course|players|games|detail`, which opens the new-round flow at that step with a sample draft (`detail` creates the sample round and opens it). The sample draft leaves the holes unscored, so the seeded rounds and the UI tests record every score. The flow also has a "Fill sample" button in debug builds. `-debugSeedRound finalPush` creates a finished round whose last holes push, to show the unresolved skins carryover; `-debugSeedStartedAt <seconds since 1970>` sets its start. `-debugSeedRound wolf` creates a four-player Wolf round scored through hole 16 with three players tied for last place, so hole 17 asks the group to pick the Wolf. `-debugSeedRound gallery` creates three rounds (one being played, one finished with every kind of score, and the one with the carryover) and stays on the list. With a seeded round, `-debugOpen scoring|settlement|hole:<n>` opens that screen of it instead of its detail. `-debugColorScheme light|dark` shows the app in that color scheme. `-debugLinkOpener opens|fails` opens no link: the link is recorded and shown on the payment sheet (`fails` behaves as if Venmo was not installed). `-debugStoreFile <name>` keeps the store in a file of its own in the temporary directory, so that a UI test can relaunch the app on what it saved, and `-debugStoreCounts` shows the number of stored rounds, holes, players and scores under the rounds list. `-debugCourseLookup fixture|off` serves the course fixtures of `CourseFixtures.swift` (Oak Glen with made-up tees) instead of the API, or no lookup at all, as in a build without the API settings; the UI tests run with it off.

The launch argument `-inMemoryStore` keeps the store in memory, so every launch starts empty; the UI tests use it. The tests run unsigned (`CODE_SIGNING_ALLOWED=NO`), which the simulator accepts for the UI test runner too.

## Course lookup

The course step of the new-round flow searches the deployed courses API (`docs/api.md`, Courses and "Deployment (dev)") and fills the course name, rating, slope and every hole's par and stroke index from the tee that is picked; the fields stay editable, and the step says which course and tee filled them. A tee with `strokeIndexValid: false`, or without 18 holes, is shown but cannot be picked. New rounds start on Oak Glen Golf Course (or the course and tee picked last on this phone): from the on-device cache when it is there, otherwise with one request in the background that fills a draft nothing has been typed into; if it cannot be fetched, only the name is filled in. "Near me" asks for When In Use location once and lists up to three courses already cached on the phone within 5 km, nearest first; nothing is picked for the group, the fix is used only for that ranking, and no coordinate is logged or sent anywhere.

The Courses tab is the same lookup outside of setup. It has the search field (debounced, three characters at least, cache first), "Near me" over the courses on the phone, and a Recent section of every course cached on the phone, most recently looked up first, so the tab works offline; without the API settings it says lookup is not configured and still lists them. A course opens from the phone's copy, whatever its age, and costs a request only when the phone does not have it. The course screen shows the address, a tee menu with the rating, slope, par and yardage of the tee, and the tee's scorecard (hole, par, stroke index, yards, with Out, In and Total rows); a tee without stroke indexes or 18 holes says so and cannot start a round. "Start a round here" opens the setup flow with the draft filled from the course and tee (`CoursesModel.draft(for:tee:)`, the same fill as the course step), and once the round is created the app switches to the Rounds tab and opens it (`AppNavigation`). `CoursesView`, `CourseDetailView`, `CoursesModel` and the `TeeScorecard` it renders are in `Courses/`.

The base URL and the `x-wad-client` token come from `Config/Local.xcconfig` (git-ignored; copy `Local.xcconfig.example`, which says where to get both) through the `WAD_API_BASE_URL` and `WAD_API_CLIENT_TOKEN` entries of the Info.plist. Without them the app builds and runs, and the step says course lookup is not configured; courses are entered by hand. CI builds without them. `Courses/` has the API client (`CourseLookupClient`), the SwiftData cache (`CourseCache`: courses and searches for 7 days, and anything cached stands in when the API cannot be reached, since the provider allows about 35 requests a day), the cache-first `CourseLookup`, the debounced `CourseSearchModel`, and `CourseStepModel`, which the course step drives. The lookup state is not stored on the round: a `Round` keeps the course name, rating, slope and holes as before.

## Payments

On a final settlement (all 18 holes scored and no greenie left unpaid) each payment can be paid or requested with Venmo and marked paid. A provisional settlement offers neither.

- One phone scores for the group, so the app offers both: "Pay with Venmo" goes to the payee's handle, "Request with Venmo" is a charge to the payer's handle. Without the handle the action is disabled and the handle can be added there.
- The links are `venmo://paycharge?txn=<pay|charge>&recipients=<handle>&amount=<dollars.cents>&note=<note>` and, when the Venmo app cannot be opened, `https://venmo.com/<handle>?txn=<pay|charge>&amount=<dollars.cents>&note=<note>`. Venmo does not document them; the sources are cited in `VenmoLink.swift`. The amount is formatted from integer cents.
- The app moves no money and does not know what happened in Venmo: it asks before marking a payment paid. A payment can also be marked paid by hand.
- A paid marker belongs to the payment with its payer, payee and amount, like the backend's transfer id (`docs/api.md`). After a score correction a marker that matches no payment is listed as recorded before a correction, marks nothing paid, and can be removed.
- No entitlement or Info.plist entry is needed: the app opens the link and looks at the result instead of asking `canOpenURL`.

## Wolf

Wolf (`docs/domain-model.md`, Game 4; ADR-0012) is played with exactly four players over 18 holes. The games step has a Wolf toggle, enabled only with four players in the draft, the value per point ($1 by default, integer cents) and the tee order, which defaults to the order the players were added and can be moved up and down there, and again from the scoring screen until the round has a score or a Wolf record. On each hole the scoring screen shows who the Wolf is (the tee order rotation on holes 1 to 16; the player in last place on points on 17 and 18), the choice (one partner or Lone Wolf, which can be corrected later), the hole's result and the running points. When players are tied for last place before 17 or 18 the engine does not pick (Open Question 4): the screen asks the group to pick the Wolf among the tied players only, and the hole shows "needs a Wolf" and scores nothing until they do. Blind Wolf is not played. The settlement shows each player's points and money (every pair settles the point difference at the value per point, from the engine's deltas), a Wolf column by game and the result of every hole; a round whose Wolf is not finished (a hole needs a Wolf, has no choice or has an invalid record) is provisional, says why, and lists the holes to fix. The engine is the only source of the points and money: nothing is scored in Swift. The settings and records are on the round (`Round.wolfPointCents`, `Round.wolfTeeOrderIDs`, `RoundHole.wolfChoice`, `wolfPartnerID` and `wolfPlayerID`).

## Updating the app

`RoundPlayer.venmoHandle`, the `PaidMarker` model, then the course cache (`CachedCourse`, `CachedCourseSearch`) and then the Wolf fields of `Round` and `RoundHole` were added to the store. SwiftData migrates an existing store by itself (a new optional attribute, a new model and a new to-many relationship), so rounds are kept when the app is updated. `StoreMigrationTests` writes a store with the previous models and opens it with the current ones. To check by hand on a simulator: run the previous build with `-debugSeedRound finalPush -debugStoreFile check`, then the new build with `-debugStoreFile check -debugStoreCounts`; the round is in the list with its 54 scores.

The bundle id is `com.gillzj00.wad` and there is no signing team yet; both are set in `project.yml`.
